## Key Mathematical Concepts Summary

### **Bayesian Learning**
- Bayes' Theorem: P(h|D) = P(D|h) × P(h) / P(D)
- MAP Hypothesis: argmax_h P(h|D) = argmax_h P(D|h) × P(h)
- ML Hypothesis: argmax_h P(D|h)
- When equal priors: MAP = ML

### **Naive Bayes**
- Conditional Independence: P(X|Y) = ∏_i P(X_i|Y)
- Parameter Estimation: P(X_i=a|Y) = Count(X_i=a ∧ Y) / Count(Y)
- Laplace Smoothing: (Count + m) / (Total + m×categories)

### **Markov Models**
- First-Order Markov: P(q_n|q_1,...,q_{n-1}) = P(q_n|q_{n-1})
- Markov Chain: λ = (π, A, Q)
- Likelihood: P(Q) = π_{q0} × ∏_i a_{i,i+1}

### **Hidden Markov Models**
- HMM: λ = (π, A, B)
- Forward: α_t(j) = [∑_i α_{t-1}(i) × a_{ij}] × b_j(o_t)
- Backward: β_t(i) = ∑_j a_{ij} × b_j(o_{t+1}) × β_{t+1}(j)
- Viterbi: δ_t(j) = max_i [δ_{t-1}(i) × a_{ij}] × b_j(o_t)
- Gamma: γ_t(j) = (α_t(j) × β_t(j)) / P(O|λ)
- Xi: ξ_t(i,j) = (α_t(i) × a_{ij} × b_j(o_{t+1}) × β_{t+1}(j)) / P(O|λ)

## Computational Complexity Summary

| Algorithm | Complexity | Space |
|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Brute Force Likelihood | O(T × N^T) | O(N^T) |
| Forward Algorithm | O(N² × T) | O(N × T) |
| Viterbi Algorithm | O(N² × T) | O(N × T) |
| Baum-Welch (per iteration) | O(N² × T) | O(N × T) |
| Naive Bayes Training | O(m × n) | O(|V| × n) |

Where: N = number of states, T = sequence length, m = training examples, n = features, |V| = vocabulary size
